# Dave Usage Policy

By using Dave (including model weights, code, or outputs), you agree to comply with the following usage restrictions. Violations may result in termination of access and legal action under applicable laws.

## ✅ Permitted Uses

- Conducting **authorized** penetration testing assessments within **United States jurisdiction only**
- Creating **isolated, non-persistent proof-of-concept demonstrations** in lab environments (never exploit code/malware)
- Writing professional reports using OWASP, DHS/CISA, NIST, MITRE, and other US-government references
- Internal use by enterprise security teams, US government contractors, or licensed professionals with written authorization

## ❌ Strictly Prohibited Uses

You **may not** use Dave to:

1.  **Target non-US systems, data, or entities** (including foreign governments, corporations, or infrastructure)
    *Rationale: Violates EAR 734.3 (Security Items), ITAR Category XIII, and OFAC sanctions.*

2.  **Develop, test, or deploy exploit code, malware, persistence mechanisms, or weaponized tools**
    *Rationale: Violates (ISC)² Canon 1 (protect society) and Canon 2 (act legally).*

3.  **Conduct security assessments without explicit, written authorization** covering specific targets, techniques, and timeframe
    *Rationale: Violates (ISC)² Canon 2 and 3, and laws like the CFAA (18 U.S.C. § 1030).*

4.  **Export or re-export the model/outputs to sanctioned countries or persons**
    *Rationale: Violates EAR § 734.3 and ITAR § 120.10.*

5.  **Produce reports that knowingly misrepresent findings, omit material facts, or violate confidentiality**
    *Rationale: Violates (ISC)² Canon 2 (honesty) and Canon 3 (diligent service).*

6.  **Assist in activities that endanger critical infrastructure, public safety, or national security**
    *Rationale: Violates (ISC)² Canon 1 and EAR 734.3.*

## 🔐 Enforcement

- Dave includes runtime checks to refuse requests violating these policies (e.g., non-US targets, missing authorization).
- By downloading or using the model, you affirm you have read, understood, and agree to these terms.
- Questions regarding usage compliance should be directed to your legal counsel.
